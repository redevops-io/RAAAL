{# The strategy selector: a browseable card grid over the whole library, with the category-then-strategy selects kept underneath as the scripting-off fallback. The dropdown made the library a wall a person scans rather than a choice they make; the card grid makes every offered strategy visible and scannable, grouped by the distinction the engine actually acts on — what puts money in and what it buys — with its research provenance beside it. Picking a card does exactly what picking from the dropdown did: the sentence lands in the box and the form is read. Two rules the library holds are preserved here. **Everything is offered; what cannot run is refused by name** — so every card is selectable and the engine answers each honestly on read. And the universe panel shows honestly what a strategy *runs on* (the fixed ten-fund research universe for the computed strategies, the funds a sentence names for the rest) rather than implying an investable-universe selection the engine does not take. Progressive enhancement: with scripting off, the two selects below are the whole selector, grouped by `optgroup`, all entries selectable, the textarea the real input — exactly the interface that was here before. With scripting on, the selects are hidden and the card grid drives the same `#pick` element, so the `picked` value and the fill-and-read behaviour are unchanged. #}
