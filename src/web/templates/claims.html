Assumptions

Every defect in this platform's erratum history was an undeclared assumption — not a wrong claim and not missing evidence. The covariance estimator, the trading calendar, the execution lag, the constraint-precedence rule and the annualization basis were each a hidden choice that moved a published number. Declaring them is what turns "which files mention EWMA?" into "which methodologies depend on this assumption?"
